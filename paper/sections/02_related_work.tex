\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Visual-inertial SLAM on micro-drones.}
ORB-SLAM3, VINS-Mono/Fusion, and OKVIS are the standard references. We
build atop a keyframe-based monocular VSLAM and treat its outputs as a
black box.

\paragraph{Perception-aware control.}
Falanga et al. and Murali et al. introduced perception-aware MPC for
quadrotors. Our contribution is the addition of a 1D-beam validity term
that encodes the assumption that the rangefinder must observe a usable
surface for scale recovery.

\paragraph{Scale recovery for monocular SLAM.}
Approaches include using IMU pre-integration, learned depth priors, and
known-size landmarks. Direct use of a single-beam rangefinder remains
under-explored despite the sensor's ubiquity on consumer drones.

\paragraph{On-device LLMs for robotics.}
Recent work (SayCan, Code-as-Policies, RT-2) couples large cloud-side
models with robots. We instead use Gemini Nano, which executes
on-device, fitting the GPS-denied operational assumption that comms are
also unreliable.
