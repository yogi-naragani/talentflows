\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Visual-inertial SLAM on micro-drones.}
ORB-SLAM3~\cite{campos2021orbslam3} and VINS-Mono / VINS-Fusion~\cite{qin2018vinsmono}
are the dominant open implementations. Recent comparative work
\cite{merzlyakov2021vslam,schmidt2025vislamloop} highlights ORB-SLAM3's
multi-map (Atlas) and loop-closure machinery as the key advantage for
missions with intermittent tracking, which fits our degradation
scenarios. We adopt ORB-SLAM3 in mono-inertial mode and report
VINS-Fusion as an ablation column.

\paragraph{Perception-aware control.}
PAMPC~\cite{falanga2018pampc} introduced perception-aware MPC for
quadrotors. Recent work extends this with sampling-based path-integral
formulations~\cite{pampi2025} and feature-limited
planning~\cite{paplanning2025}. These efforts assume the SLAM
front-end keeps working; \emph{none co-design with a high-level
supervisor that recognizes degradation and changes mission intent}.
We address this gap by reweighting the perception cost at runtime in
response to advisories from an on-device LLM.

\paragraph{Scale recovery for monocular SLAM.}
Approaches include IMU pre-integration, learned depth priors, and
known-size landmarks. Direct use of a single-beam range sensor has
been shown for scale correction~\cite{zhang2018rangefinderscale} and
visual-LiDAR fusion~\cite{vlidaroscale2023}, but typically only as a
correction signal during nominal operation. Our 1D rangefinder also
acts as a clearance fallback when vision fails.

\paragraph{Onboard acoustic sensing on drones.}
Most onboard drone-audition research treats propeller ego-noise as a
\emph{nuisance} to suppress~\cite{rotorconditioned2025}. BatDeck
demonstrated active ultrasonic obstacle avoidance on a
nano-drone~\cite{batdeck2024}, and Fontaine et al.~\cite{fontaine2021}
showed that ego-noise reflections can localize acoustic reflectors
\emph{from a stationary robot}. To our knowledge, no published flying
micro-drone uses passive ego-noise reflectometry as an onboard
SLAM-degradation backup; the closest active analogue is BatDeck. Our
contribution differs by being passive (no emitter, no extra payload,
shared mic with audition tasks) and by activating only on SLAM-health
alarms.

\paragraph{On-device LLMs for robotics.}
SayCan, Code-as-Policies, and RT-2 couple large cloud-side models with
robots. On-device deployments have so far been used as
natural-language command translators~\cite{px4ollama2025} or task-level
planners~\cite{llmland2025}. Google DeepMind's recent ``Gemini
Robotics On-Device''~\cite{geminirobotics2025} announcement shows the
direction. Our use is distinct: Gemini Nano runs at 1--5\,Hz as a
real-time \emph{advisory} layer reasoning over SLAM / IMU / acoustic
health to adapt the MPC, not to issue low-level commands. We are
careful to distinguish the on-device Gemini Nano SLM from the larger
``Gemini Robotics'' model.

\paragraph{GPS-denied UAV surveys.}
The 2025 Jarraya et al.\ review~\cite{jarraya2025gpsdenied} explicitly
calls hybrid multi-sensor fusion the open frontier. Our reproducible
ROS\,2 + Gazebo testbed with degradation injection contributes a
benchmark in that direction.
