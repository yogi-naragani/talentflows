\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
We evaluate in Gazebo Harmonic (\texttt{gz-sim 8}) bridged to ROS 2 via
\texttt{ros\_gz\_bridge}. Four scenarios are tested: warehouse corridor,
forest clearing, indoor SLAM dropout (textures masked mid-flight), and
low-texture wall. Each scenario is run for 60\,s with synchronized
30\,Hz monocular images, 50\,Hz 1D range, and 200\,Hz IMU. Nodes are
launched via \texttt{ros2 launch gps\_denied\_drone sim.launch.py}.

\subsection{Ablations}
We compare five configurations: full system; no range; no
perception-cost; no reasoner; classical-only. Configuration files are
in \texttt{experiments/configs/ablations.yaml}.

\subsection{Metrics}
Absolute trajectory error (ATE-RMSE), mission completion rate, minimum
clearance, MPC solve time, and Gemini Nano latency.
