\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We described and scaffolded a navigation stack for GPS-denied drones
that combines monocular visual SLAM, a 1D range sensor, a
perception-aware MPC, and an on-device Gemini Nano reasoner. The key
design choice is to keep the hard real-time path classical and
verifiable while letting the small on-device LLM operate as a low-rate
advisor under hard safety bounds. Future work: hardware deployment on a
Tensor-class flight computer and a comparative study against
cloud-tethered LLM reasoners under intermittent comms.
